### Project Description
NEGATIVE RUNNER is a completely unnecessary 3D endless-running game where getting worse means winning. Instead of increasing your score, every mistake, obstacle, and failed move pushes your score further into the negatives. The goal is simple: run, fail, and achieve the lowest score possible in the most unnecessarily entertaining way.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Modern games have created a serious problem: players are constantly pressured to achieve high scores, beat records, and become better. But what if someone wants to be absolutely terrible at a game?

NEGATIVE RUNNER solves this completely unnecessary problem by giving players a game where failure is rewarded. Instead of chasing a positive score, players must reach the lowest possible negative score. Finally, there is a game where making mistakes, hitting obstacles, and performing badly can make you a winner.
### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We created NEGATIVE RUNNER — a completely unnecessary 3D endless runner where failure is the ultimate achievement. Players run through obstacles, collect negative points, and try to make their score as low as possible.

Forget high scores, winning streaks, and becoming a better player. Here, the worse you perform, the more successful you become. Because sometimes, going backwards is the only way forward. 😎
